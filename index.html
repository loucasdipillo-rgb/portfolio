About me

Finance meets
sustainability.

Master's student in Macroeconomics & Finance at HEC Liège, combining a rigorous quantitative background — econometrics, Python, NLP — with a strong focus on sustainable finance and ESG research. Trilingual (French, English, Italian), with international experience including a semester at Lakeland University (Wisconsin, USA).

My thesis investigates whether mandatory ESG disclosure under the EU's NFRD/CSRD reduced the cost of equity for European firms, using a firm-level identification strategy based on the 500-employee reporting threshold and a staggered difference-in-differences design (Callaway & Sant'Anna, 2021). Alongside that, my academic work spans portfolio management, macroeconomic modelling, NLP-based return prediction, and ESG disclosure analysis — while I manage a personal passive ETF portfolio and stay closely engaged with financial markets.

Open to end-of-studies internship opportunities from February 2027 in finance, ESG research, or strategy — preferably in Luxembourg or Geneva.

Drivers of Market Fear: VIX vs VSTOXX

Applied Econometrics — HEC Liège, 2024–2025 · Co-authored with Lucas Deweys

Panel study identifying the macroeconomic and financial drivers of implied volatility in the US (VIX) and Euro Area (VSTOXX), covering 2015–2024. Two-way fixed effects with HAC-robust corrections, interaction terms for regional heterogeneity, and a pooled logit model estimating the probability of entering a high-fear regime.

Predicting S&P 500 Reactions from Fed Speeches

Data Management — HEC Liège

NLP pipeline predicting short-term S&P 500 direction from 1,385 Fed Governor speeches (1996–2020). Merged Kaggle speech data with Yahoo Finance prices, applied the Loughran-McDonald sentiment lexicon, and tested logistic regression across t+1, t+3, t+5 horizons. Accuracy rises to 67% at t+5 — consistent with gradual information incorporation under efficient markets.

Environmental Regulation & Pollution-Haven Trade

International Trade Economics — HEC Liège, December 2025

Gravity model panel study testing the pollution-haven hypothesis: do stricter environmental policies in high-income countries boost exports of pollution-intensive goods from LMICs? Bilateral trade panel (2010–2018) merging UN Comtrade, OECD EPS index, World Bank WDI, and CEPII GeoDist. Log-linear model with exporter, importer & year fixed effects and cluster-robust SEs.
